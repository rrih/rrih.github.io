Data and asset sources

Pokémon information and artwork come from PokéAPI and PokéAPI sprites. Model sources include Pokémon HOME GLB Models, Pokémon HOME Unity Models, and Pokémon 3D API assets. The app incorporates original idle animations and adapts color, transparency, and other appearance details for web display. Some special visual effects are approximations.

Rights requests

For concerns about asset rights, contact us through GitHub Issues. Identify the affected material and your relationship to the rights holder, using information you can safely make public. We will review the report and reconsider the material where appropriate. GitHub Issues are public; do not post private information or confidential documents.

Preview and promotional images

The illustration used to introduce the app as a whole was created with OpenAI image generation. Individual Pokémon and form previews are still images rendered from the included models. Reference artwork is identified when used instead.

Supplementary Pokédex descriptions and categories

Some Japanese descriptions are concise Atlas summaries based on the official Japanese Pokédex, identified separately from game Pokédex entries. Some Chinese category names are supplemented from the official Simplified Chinese Pokédex and Traditional Chinese Pokédex. Where a localized description is unavailable, Atlas presents a factual summary of types, height and weight. Each page identifies its source and verification date.
