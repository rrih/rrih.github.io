About Pokémon Atlas

You do not need an Atlas account. Available models, forms, animations, and features may change. The app includes visual adjustments and approximations; it does not promise an exact reproduction of an official game's appearance or behavior.

Your images and saved scenes

Choose background images you are entitled to use, respecting other people's copyright, image rights, and privacy. The app processes and saves backgrounds on your device. Selecting an image does not publish it or send it to the operator. The operator does not acquire a right to use your image in advertising or promotion.

Downloading an image or scene does not transfer any third-party rights. Before publishing or sending an exported file elsewhere, check the rights to its images and characters, permission from any people shown, and whether it contains personal information.

Scenes, favorites, and backgrounds are saved for use in that browser or device. They do not automatically sync between devices. Browser data deletion, storage limits, or device failure may remove them. Export scenes you want to keep using the available save tools. A scene file exported with its background includes that image; sending the file to another device also sends the image.
