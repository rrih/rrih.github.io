About Pokémon Atlas

Pokémon Atlas is an independent fan-made 3D Pokédex operated by rrih. You can explore Pokémon models, compare available appearances, arrange a scene and save it on your device.

Contact

For a problem, correction, accessibility issue or rights-related request, open an issue in the site repository. Include the page URL and a short description. GitHub issues are public and require a GitHub account. Do not include personal information, private images or account credentials.

The operator’s public profile is github.com/rrih. The site’s source and published updates are available in the repository.

Data and credits

Pokémon data is based on PokéAPI and the sources linked on individual pages. When a description is an Atlas summary or a factual summary, its source label distinguishes it from a game Pokédex entry. The collection includes the 1,025 numbered species and the alternate forms currently listed in the forms catalog. Model detail and recorded animations vary.

Pokémon Atlas is not an official service of, and is not affiliated with, Nintendo, Creatures, GAME FREAK or The Pokémon Company. Pokémon names, characters, artwork and other third-party materials belong to their respective rights holders. See Rights & credits for the full asset credits and notices.

Your choices

Scenes and background images are stored locally. Analytics requires your optional consent, and advertising choices are separate. See the Privacy policy and Terms of use.
